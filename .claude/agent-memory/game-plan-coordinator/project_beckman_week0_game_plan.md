Drafted `Football/game-plans/week-0-beckman.md` on 2026-08-20 — the first plan produced by this
system. Status left as **DRAFT**: the cross-model review never ran (Bash denied), so no reviewer
objections exist yet and Xander has arbitrated nothing.

**Why:** Xander logged two Beckman games (36 entries total, sessions `bb52a4e2` and `92718f87`,
both `week: "0"`, filmDate 2026-08-13) and pasted his coaches' Beckman *offensive* scouting report.
Both source films were charted **entirely from the Beckman-offense side — zero defensive reps** — so
the defensive prep sheet is real and the offensive prep sheet is an honest empty section with a
charting to-do list instead of invented coverages.

**Tendencies found (all small-sample):** Dubs/2x2 → pass 10 of 11; Wing → run 4 of 6 with both
passes off a stretch fake; Wing/stretch → fake-stretch → Y in the flat, 3 reps (one logged verbatim
as "Naked", an exact deck match); backside fade away from strength, 4 reps; compressed splits →
crossers, 3 reps; motion side = throw side, 3 of 3; 2-back → a back releases, 2 of 3; 1st down was
70% pass/screen.

**The weakness to re-check first if Beckman comes up again:** the two games barely overlap. All 4
fade reps are from Buena Park and 0 from SHHS/BPHS; all 3 crosser reps are from SHHS/BPHS and 0 from
Buena Park. **These may be per-opponent game plans rather than Beckman identity.** More film settles
it — that was open question #2 on the coach-questions list.

**Also unresolved:** file-naming convention (used `week-0-beckman.md` to match the sessions' `week`
field; the `game-plans/` folder did not exist and was created), and whether the coaches' "72% strong
side" means formation strength or field width.

**How to apply:** re-read the session files fresh before any follow-up plan — do not carry these
counts forward as fact. If Beckman is scouted again, the first job is checking whether the fade and
crossers patterns hold across a third game.
