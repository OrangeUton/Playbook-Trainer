Xander is a high school football player (not a coach) who logs his own opponent film in the Film Log
app and brings the resulting prep sheet into coach conversations.

- **He plays both ways.** His own words: "I'm going to need to be ready for both offense and
  defense." Never hard-code a game plan to one side.
- **As a receiver**, the knowledge base's §4 pre-snap ladder and receiver conversion rules are the
  parts written *for him* — frame offensive prep in terms of his own read (safety count, his
  corner's leverage/eyes), not in coordinator language.
- **He gets a real coaches' scouting report** separately from his own logging. When both exist,
  treat them as two independent sources and mark where they agree, conflict, or don't touch. The
  coaches' report outranks anything derived here — they chart things the Film Log has no field for.
- **He is comfortable with honest gaps.** He supplied a small sample himself ("It's not a ton of
  data") and expects that acknowledged, not papered over. Stating "3 reps is 3 reps" or "this
  section is empty and here's why" is the expected output, not a failure.
